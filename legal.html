Privacy policy

AFE Charge uses your email and password verifier to secure your account. If provided, your name, South African mobile number, vehicle details and preferred vehicle help manage charging. Peach handles card entry. If you consent to save a card, AFE stores a payment token, brand and last four digits. AFE links your order and vehicle to the charging session and exchanges the relevant charging reference and connector identifiers with the operator.

You can view and correct your profile, remove vehicles and unlink saved cards in the app. Before launch, the operator must publish its responsible party, Information Officer contact, processing grounds, recipients, cross-border processing and retention periods, and the process to exercise privacy rights or lodge a complaint.

Terms of service

Choose your vehicle and connector. Review the tariff and the amount of any card hold before authorising payment. A hold temporarily reserves funds. Command acceptance and the charger actually dispensing energy are recorded separately. Commercial tariffs, billing, cancellation, refunds and support details must be finalised before paid service opens.

Delete your AFE Charge account

Sign in below, enter your current password and confirm permanent deletion. In the app you can also choose Profile → Account security → Delete account. End any active charge and refresh its status first.

Your sign-in, profile, vehicles, saved card links and personal order list are removed. Charging and payment records needed for reconciliation or statutory obligations may be retained. The commercial retention schedule and support contact are pending.

隐私政策

AFE Charge 使用邮箱和密码验证信息保护账户。你可填写姓名、南非手机号及车辆信息，用于管理账户和选择充电车辆。银行卡资料由 Peach 收银台采集。经你同意保存卡片后，AFE 保存支付令牌、品牌和末四位。AFE 通过订单关联账户、车辆和充电会话，并与场站运营方交换充电参考号及枪口标识。

你可在 App 内查询或更正资料，移除车辆、解绑卡片。商业上线前需公布责任方、信息官联系方式、处理依据、数据接收方、跨境处理、保存期限，并说明如何行使个人信息权利和提出投诉。

服务条款

充电前请选择车辆和枪口，确认费率、预授权金额及支付方式。预授权会临时冻结相应额度。平台受理启停指令后，实际充电状态以桩端上报的会话为准。商业费率、结算、取消、退款和客服条款将在收费服务开放前公布。

注销 AFE Charge 账户

通过下方入口登录，输入当前密码并确认永久注销。也可在 App 内选择“我的 → 账户安全 → 注销账户”。如正在充电，请先结束并刷新订单状态。

注销将删除登录信息、资料、车辆、绑卡关联和个人订单列表。因对账或法定义务需保留的充电和支付记录可能继续保存。商业版的具体保留期限和客服联系方式待公布。
